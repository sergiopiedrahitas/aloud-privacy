Aloud

The read-along audiobook player for the audiobooks you already own.

Your library, wherever it lives

  • On your phone or SD card — grant a folder once with Android's system picker and Aloud finds every book in it.
  • In your Google Drive — connect your account, point Aloud at your audiobooks folder, and stream your library without filling your phone. Read-only access; Aloud never changes anything in your Drive.
  • Free classics — browse and stream thousands of public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox.

Listen and read, together

  • Read-along — Aloud can transcribe a book on your device, or pair a free Project Gutenberg ebook, and highlight the text word by word as the narrator speaks.
  • Real chapters — embedded M4B/MP3 chapter support, multi-file books, smart title and author detection, covers and ratings.
  • Made for listening — lock-screen controls, sleep timer, playback speed, bookmarks with typed or spoken notes.
Private by architecture. Aloud has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything — your library, your progress, your transcripts, your notes — stays on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
